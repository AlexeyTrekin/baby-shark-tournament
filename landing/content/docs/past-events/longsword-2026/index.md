rules_content: |
  ### Categories

  - Longsword - beginners

  ### Admission Rules

  - No more than 2 years of HEMA experience
  - No medals or record of major success (like top-16 at big tournaments) from any HEMA tournaments
  - No medals from related sports (tap fight, buhurt, olympic fencing)

  ### Competition Rules

  This tournament followed the [Swordplay-2026 rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eBtnbKMbdQPTbPYh5-t6KuS_U3qYtH1zsD1KUGKzZXA/) (Longsword section) with some changes for better experience and safety of the beginners:

  1. One-handed strikes do not count at all
  2. Grappling is not allowed, the exchange is stopped immediately
  3. More cautious approach to safety violations and excessive force rules, lower intensity than usually

  ### Gear Requirements

  Same as [Swordplay-2026 gear requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TNqSz8FNsuRTq0vb67ITqsCLnRkWSmBQezVIe6JxMqM) with exception of 350N-rated jackets and pants being NOT mandatory.

The **Baby Shark: Longsword 2026** took place on **24.10.2026** at the **Flavium hall**, sharing the venue with the joint training of the tournament staff for Swordplay 2026.

The tournament was fully booked: 16 participants fought in the beginners' longsword category.

### Thank you!

Thank you to all participants for the great fights and the good spirit! We hope the experience you gained here will help you in your future tournaments.

Huge thanks to the whole tournament staff: the judges, the scorekeepers and the gear check team, who volunteered their time to make this event happen. This event would not be possible without you!
