  ## Categories

  - Sabre and sidesword (mixed) - beginners

  ## Admission Rules

  The admission rules are the same as previously:

  - No more than 2 years of HEMA experience
  - No medals or record of major success (like top-16 at big tournaments) from any HEMA tournaments
  - No medals from related sports (tap fight, buhurt, olympic fencing)

  ## Rules

  Sabre and sidesword fighters compete together. We will use the rules of Swordplay-2026 tournament (see Sidesword and Saber sections, they have the same fight rules).

  [Swordplay-2026 rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eBtnbKMbdQPTbPYh5-t6KuS_U3qYtH1zsD1KUGKzZXA/).

  To create better experience and safety for the beginners, we have the following difference to the Swordplay ruleset:
  1. Grappling is not allowed, the exchange will be stopped immediately
  2. We will be more cautious to safety violations and excessive force rules applications, so aim for lower intensity than usually.

  ## Gear Requirements

  Same as Swordplay-2026 (see Sidesword and Saber sections) with exception of 350N-rated jackets and pants being NOT mandatory here.

  If you have doubts in your gear, or lack some parts, [contact us](mailto:baby.shark.hema@gmail.com), we can help you to find something.

  See the [gear requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TNqSz8FNsuRTq0vb67ITqsCLnRkWSmBQezVIe6JxMqM).
